# Privacy Policy – Pure Tone

**App:** Pure Tone (Android package `com.jcdr.puretone`)
**Developer:** Jean-Christian de Rivaz (Google Play developer name: jcdr)
**Contact:** puretone.support@gmail.com
**Effective date:** 30 September 2026

## Summary

Pure Tone is an offline tone generator. It plays a sine wave through your device's audio output. **The app does not collect, store, share, or send any personal data.**

## What the app does not do

Pure Tone:

- requests **no Android permissions**. It does not request Internet or network access, so it cannot send data off your device;
- has **no user accounts** and no sign-in;
- contains **no advertising**, **no analytics**, and **no crash-reporting** or tracking software, and it does not use the Android advertising ID;
- does **not** access your microphone, camera, contacts, location, photos, files, or other personal information;
- does **not** save your settings. Frequency and amplitude go back to their defaults each time the app starts.

## Information processed only on your device

- **Audio output devices:** while it is running, the app checks the list of audio output devices (for example the speaker, wired headphones, or Bluetooth headphones). It uses this list only to restart the sound when you change devices. This information stays in memory on your device and is never saved or sent anywhere.
- **Technical logs:** the app writes short technical messages about the audio system (for example "audio stream started") to the standard Android system log on your device. These messages contain no personal data, and the app never sends them anywhere.

## Google Play

When you download Pure Tone from Google Play, Google may process information about the install, updates, and (if you have enabled it on your device) crash or performance statistics. Google processes this under its own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy), not through Pure Tone's code. The developer may see only aggregated, anonymous statistics that Google provides in the Play Console.

## Contacting support

If you email puretone.support@gmail.com, your email address and message are used only to reply to you. They are not shared with anyone else or used for marketing, and they are deleted when they are no longer needed to handle your request.

## Children

Pure Tone is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Your rights

Pure Tone collects no personal data, so the app itself holds nothing about you to access, correct, or delete. If you have emailed support, you can ask at any time for your messages to be deleted. This applies under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and similar laws.

## Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles data (for example, if network access were added), this policy will be updated and its effective date changed before that version is released.
