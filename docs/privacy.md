# Privacy Policy — Pure Tone

**Publisher:** jcdr  
**App:** Pure Tone (`com.jcdr.puretone`)  
**Last updated:** 2026-07-18

## Summary

Pure Tone is a minimal offline tool that generates an audio sine wave on your device. It is designed so that **no personal data needs to leave your phone** for the app to work.

## Data collection

Pure Tone does **not**:

- require an account or login  
- include advertising SDKs  
- include analytics or crash-reporting SDKs  
- sell or share personal information with third parties  

The app generates sound locally using the device audio system. Frequency and amplitude are controlled only on the device.

## Internet

The core app does not need network access for tone generation. If you install Pure Tone from Google Play, Google’s own services process install and update data under Google’s policies—not under this app’s code.

## Permissions

Pure Tone aims to use only what is required for on-device audio playback. It does not request access to contacts, location, microphone recording, photos, or similar personal data for its core features.

## Children

Pure Tone is a general utility tool. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect children’s data.

## Changes

If this policy changes in a material way (for example if analytics were ever added), the app listing and this document will be updated.

## Contact

For privacy questions about Pure Tone, contact the publisher **jcdr** at **puretone.support@gmail.com**.
